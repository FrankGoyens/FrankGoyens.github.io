**Last updated:** August 15th 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit **https://frankgoyens.github.io**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Email Newsletter
If you voluntarily subscribe to our mailing list, we collect your email address. We use this solely to send updates, announcements, and news regarding our apps and games. 

* **Service Provider:** We use **Mailchimp** to manage and send our email newsletters. Your email address is securely stored on Mailchimp's servers.
* **Opting Out:** You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any email we send.

### B. Analytics & Usage Data
We use **Google Analytics** to understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., pages viewed, time spent on site, device type, approximate geographic location). 

* Google Analytics uses **cookies** and similar tracking technologies to collect non-personally identifiable usage data.
* You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
* You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### C. Feedback Data
If users submit a bug report or feedback, you process the message text, optional contact info, and attached files (such as screenshots) strictly to review their input or fix issues.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with service providers necessary to operate our website:

* **GitHub Pages:** For hosting the website.
* **Mailchimp:** For storing email lists and delivering newsletters.
* **Google Analytics:** For website traffic statistics.
* **FormSubmit:** Used as a backend service to relay user feedback, bug reports, and file attachments from website forms to our email inbox. FormSubmit processes form data strictly to deliver these email notifications.

---

## 3. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Under privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:
* Access the personal data we hold about you.
* Request the complete deletion of your email address from our mailing list.

To request data removal or ask questions about this policy, contact us at: **frank.goyens@geeapp.info**
